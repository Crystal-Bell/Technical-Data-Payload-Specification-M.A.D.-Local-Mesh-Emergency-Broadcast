1. Market Opportunity & Addressable Sectors
The haptic bio-feedback tether bridges three multi-billion-dollar global markets currently dominated by single-purpose, closed-ecosystem devices:
 * Advanced Pet Tech & Safety: Premium pet tracking, gentle-training, and health-monitoring collars. (Global pet tech market exceeding $7 billion).
 * Assistive Technology & Sensory Substitution: Wearable spatial navigation aids for the visually impaired and elderly. (Global assistive tech market scaling past $30 billion).
 * Industrial & Field Worker Safety: Fatigue-monitoring, proximity-warning, and hands-free communication gear for remote, high-risk operators (forestry, construction, rescue).

3. Monetization & Value-Capture Strategy (The "Open Core" Model)
To maintain open-source humanitarian standards while ensuring sustainable project continuation, market capture relies on a tiered value structure:
 * The Public Commons (Free / Unlicense): All foundational schematics, basic firmware code, and 3D print files are 100% free and open for public download, modification, and self-manufacturing.
 * Pre-Assembled Hardware Kits (Low-Cost Retail): Offering pre-calibrated component kits, medical-grade silicone straps, and high-durability rare-earth magnetic mounts for users who prefer out-of-the-box hardware over DIY assembly.
 * Enterprise & Fleet Integration Packs: Custom mesh routing packages for search-and-rescue teams, multi-dog kennels, or municipal accessibility programs needing robust scaling.
